Why this hits close to home for every Canadian

More and more people are choosing Canadian goods over American ones, even when they cost a bit more. That definitely helps, but the reality goes a lot deeper:

  • As of September 2026, 64.6% of RRSP and TFSA assets are actually invested in the US market.
  • Most cars—take the Honda Civic, for example—are half-made in the US, even if the label says Made in Canada.
  • Google, Facebook, YouTube, Reddit, our electronics, computers, and phones—all of it was designed or built in the US.

And there are thousands of examples just like that.

That doesn’t mean you should give up on your retirement savings, send your car to the scrap heap, and switch to communicating by Canadian-bred carrier pigeons.

Canadian Tire has been around since 1922. We consider ourselves proud patriots, but even we can’t completely “cut out everything American.”

Still, we wanted to do something genuinely good for our country and its people—and that’s Canadian Tire Invest.

How Canadian Tire Invest works — and how you make money

The platform was developed in partnership with RBC and tech giant BlackBerry. It is an artificial intelligence designed by BlackBerry that automatically executes trades on the stock market.

Canadian Tire Invest’s AI responds instantly to all breaking news and Canadian and U.S. government policy decisions regarding tariffs and other global developments.

📉 The US sharply raises tariffs on alcohol from Canada — alcohol stocks drop. Our AI reacts instantly.

📈 The US raises tariffs on groceries — Loblaw stock rises, as most of its brands are Canadian-sourced. Our AI captures the move.

And so on. While it makes sense from a common-sense perspective, no asset can keep rising or falling in price forever following a particular news story or event. That is why determining which asset to sell, at what price, in what quantity, and when remains an endless headache for traders—but not for artificial intelligence.

Between January and August 2026, a study was conducted comparing the performance of professional traders against Canadian Tire Invest. Our artificial intelligence outperformed them by 2.5 times.
