      After Divorce, One Household Becomes Two. Your Paycheque Still Has to Carry a Life

      WINS launches a new AI-platform created to help 50,000 Canadian women begin rebuilding their savings and make up to $1,000 a day from home - without adding another job to an already full life.

      The platform is designed to build real savings - without relying only on traditional banks.

      Karen Ramchuk, CEO of WINS, says the initiative was built around one question: what does a woman actually need after a divorce? (CBC News)

      Megan Fraser · CBC News ·

      According to Karen Ramchuk , CEO of WINS , Profit Storm represents a key initiative aligned with her vision for a more inclusive, innovative, and accessible Canadian economy.

      The platform’s development involved key players such as RBC and TD Bank , which provided secure digital infrastructure focused on ease of use.

      It also received support from the Government of Canada and the Department of Innovation, Science and Economic Development, which ensured a clear regulatory framework.

      Input from leading Canadian universities added technical expertise and financial literacy resources, so the system stays accessible for women without any background in finance.

      This collaboration between public, private, and academic sectors aims not to benefit only a select few, but to offer millions of Canadian women a real opportunity to rebuild their financial stability on their own terms.

      Divorce Changes More Than a Relationship. It Changes the Cost of Everyday Life

      When one household becomes two, many expenses don’t disappear - they multiply.

      Housing, utilities, childcare, insurance and unexpected bills may now depend on a single income. Even women with stable jobs can feel financially exposed, simply because there is less room for anything to go wrong.

      That’s why many are looking for ways to build an additional financial cushion without giving up more of the time they already don’t have.

      This government-supported initiative was created to give divorced Canadian women access to modern financial tools once available only to large institutions - with technology powered by Profit Storm .

      The Problem Isn’t That Women Can’t Handle Money. It’s That She Has to Handle Everything

      After divorce, the same mortgage, groceries, childcare, insurance and repairs may suddenly depend on one income.

      She keeps working, paying the bills and holding life together, while quietly wondering what happens if something goes wrong.

      A car repair or a late support payment can disrupt the entire month.

      She doesn’t need another side hustle - she needs money to stop feeling like another full-time job.

      Women Across Canada Are Quietly Rebuilding

      Teachers. Nurses. Office managers. Small-business owners. Retail workers. Caregivers.

      Different jobs, provinces and divorce stories - but many want the same things:

      • savings that belong to them;
      • more control over financial decisions;
      • room for unexpected expenses;
      • progress without working every evening;
      • a future that doesn’t depend on an ex-partner.

      They aren’t chasing luxury or overnight transformation. They’re looking for financial tools that can work alongside their regular income without taking more time away from their families.

      A Way to Move Forward Without Adding More to Your Plate

      WINS offers a simpler way to begin building something of your own, without becoming a financial expert.

      The platform uses advanced automation and AI technology to help Canadian women grow savings through market analysis - without requiring financial experience.

      • ✔ Designed for women
      • ✔ Simple onboarding process
      • ✔ Personal guidance after registration
      • ✔ Accessible starting deposit

      This is not about becoming a trader.

      It’s about making your money work quietly in the background.

      Built for Women Who Already Have Enough to Manage

      The platform was developed within a regulated Canadian framework and focuses on transparency, accessibility, and responsible financial participation.

      Users keep control over their funds and can monitor progress at any time.

      No complicated financial knowledge required.

      The Goal Isn’t Luxury. It’s Knowing One Unexpected Bill Won’t Undo Everything

      Participants say the biggest benefit isn’t luxury.

      It’s breathing room.

      Being able to:

      • handle unexpected bills;
      • reduce financial stress;
      • stop living paycheque to paycheque;
      • finally start building a real safety cushion.

      “I Wasn’t Broke. I Was Just One Expense Away From Panicking.”

      “After my divorce, I stayed in our Calgary home so the kids wouldn’t have to change schools. I was working full-time and paying the bills, but one car repair or home expense could throw off the entire month.

      I started cautiously, with an amount that didn’t affect our regular budget. For the first time in years, I felt like I was building something for my own future.”

      “I didn’t need someone to rescue me. I needed a way to move forward on my own.”

      Because of growing interest, access is limited to a certain number of participants at a time to maintain support quality.

      Registration remains open while spots are available.

      Starting Doesn’t Mean Committing Today

      You don’t need financial experience, hours of free time or a fully developed investment plan to learn more.

      Getting started takes only a few minutes.

      1. Tell us where you’re starting from. Complete a short registration form with your basic contact information. No complicated financial questionnaire at the first step.
      2. Get a personal walkthrough. A platform specialist will contact you and explain how the service works in clear, everyday language. You can ask questions and take the time you need to understand the process.
      3. Decide what feels right for you. Review the information and decide whether you want to activate an account and what starting amount fits your situation. The decision remains yours.

      You’re not expected to know everything about financial markets.

      You’re expected to make the decision that is right for you.

      You’ve already started over once.

      Your financial future can start here.
